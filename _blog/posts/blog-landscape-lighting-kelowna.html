What good lighting does for your yard

  • Safer steps. Dark stairs and grade changes are how people get hurt. Built-in step lights fix that without floodlight glare.
  • A patio you use after dinner. The Okanagan gives you warm evenings. Lighting turns your patio into a second living room.
  • Your stonework stays on show. You paid for the walls and the planting. After sunset nobody can see them. Uplighting brings them back.
  • A front of house that looks as good at ten at night as it does at noon.
