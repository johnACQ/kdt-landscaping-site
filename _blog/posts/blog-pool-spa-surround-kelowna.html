Pool & spa surrounds · Kelowna

Your pool gets the attention. The surround takes the beating.

You picked your pool or hot tub with care. The ground around it takes the wet feet, the splash, the chairs and the runoff every time it rains. Laid on a poor base, it shows: stones sink near the edge, lips catch your toe, and water sits where you walk. It is almost always a base problem, not a stone problem.

If your lot slopes, your pool is a "complex and dynamic" job, the words Justin Norbraten used about his project in his review of KDT. He also wrote that KDT "handles these challenges with ease". On a slope, walls, grade and drainage all have to work at once.

Base first, drainage first

Here is what makes your surround last:

  • A packed base. The ground around a pool has often been dug up and filled back in. Stone laid on it without packing it down will settle.
  • Water sent the right way. Your surface should slope away from the pool and away from your house, so splash and rain drain off instead of pooling.
  • Flat ground first. On a slope, room for your pool or hot tub can mean a retaining wall before anything else.
  • Light on the steps. Dark stairs and grade changes are how people get hurt. Built-in step lights fix that without floodlight glare.
