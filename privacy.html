Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 July 2026

K.D.T. Landscaping Ltd. ("KDT Landscaping", "we", "us") operates kdtlandscape.ca and provides landscape construction services in Kelowna and the Okanagan. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: name, phone number, email address, the town your property is in, and what you are looking to have built.
  • Details collected automatically when you use our site: pages viewed, referring source, and standard analytics data.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a site visit or estimate.
  • To confirm, reschedule or follow up on appointments.
  • To ask for feedback after work is completed.
  • To improve our website and our advertising.

SMS and text messaging

If you provide your phone number and give consent, we may send you text messages about your enquiry, your appointment, or a request for feedback after your project.

  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any service from us.
  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help, or call us on 250-258-7125.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS consent with anyone for their own marketing. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Any sharing with service providers is strictly to help us deliver our own messages to you.

Full terms are set out in our SMS Terms.

Who we share information with

We use service providers to run our business, such as our customer relationship management system, our messaging provider, and website analytics. They may only use your information to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Our website uses Google Analytics and Google Ads. These set cookies in your browser that tell us how people found the site and which pages they used, and they let us see which of our ads led to an enquiry. This is counting and measurement, not a profile of you as a person, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

When you send us an enquiry we also share a scrambled version of your email address and phone number with Google, so it can tell us that the enquiry came from one of our ads. The scrambling is one way and happens in your browser, so Google receives a string of characters rather than your actual details, and it is used only to match your enquiry to an ad click.

You can clear or block these cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not stop you using the site or contacting us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymise them.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP, and out of emails using the unsubscribe link.
